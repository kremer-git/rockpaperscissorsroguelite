# Upgrade report

Main measure: the upgrade is **granted at round 0** to the policy matching its tree (800 runs), compared with the same policy without it. Survival to store 6 (round 32) and median rounds are used because means are dominated by rare 1000-round runs. Granting at round 0 exaggerates upgrades that shine early (charges that recharge every store), so rarity/price is part of the verdict.
